# Q3 2026 business review

> [!NOTE]
> All figures in thousands of euros (k€) unless stated. Q3 is not yet audited.[^audit]

## Summary

- **Revenue €4,820k**, up 12% on Q3 2025 and 3% above plan.
- **Gross margin 61.4%**, up 1.8 points – lower hosting costs.
- **Cash €9.1m** at the end of September, down €540k in the quarter.
- One risk to watch: the two largest customers make up 31% of revenue.

as zero.
